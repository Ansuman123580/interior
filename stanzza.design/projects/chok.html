Client:

Chök is The Chocolate Kitchen, a Barcelona hybrid of a coffee shop, a chocolate shop and a laboratory where chocolate works as a field for experiment.

Objective:

Adapt the existing offline cafe design for the Saudi market without losing the brand's positioning and visual language.

Solution:

We left the bearers with everything that already works for the brand: the ö brand name, the poster of The Chocolate Kitchen, Wishbone chairs, white fluted surfaces, light oak and a cream palette. A local layer is built on this base — Chök reads like Chök at first glance, but feels like its own in the Saudi context.
