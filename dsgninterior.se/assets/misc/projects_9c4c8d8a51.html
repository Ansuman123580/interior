Projects

Selected projects and references in interior architecture for offices, hotels and public environments.

  • Fellowmind Göteborg

    Fellowmind’s office in Gothenburg is designed as a calm and flexible work environment where clarity, functionality, and attention to detail come together. Shaped by the specific conditions of the site, the office supports focus, collaboration, and the changing rhythms of everyday work, in a setting where light, materiality, and spatial structure create a cohesive and welcoming whole.

  • Car.info

    Office interior design project for Car.info, focused on sustainable design, reuse and Scandinavian aesthetics, created by Modus Interior.

  • Stretch Care

    Stretch Care’s new office brings energy and colour to a historic building in the heart of Malmö. By allowing the organisation’s values to meet the architecture’s heritage, the project creates a contemporary workplace where materiality, expression and well-being come together. A space shaped for people, identity and everyday presence.

  • Fellowmind Stockholm

    Fellowmind’s office in Stockholm is a place where calm, clarity and function come together in a warm and welcoming work environment. Here, Modus Interior has created an office that reflects the organisation’s values through considered material choices, soft forms and a clear sense of long-term thinking.

  • Fellowmind Malmö

    Fellowmind’s office in Malmö is a warm and considered workplace where materiality, daylight and spatial structure create a clear, human-centred whole.

  • Fellowmind Jönköping

    Fellowmind’s office in Jönköping is characterised by a welcoming and approachable work environment where everyday interaction plays a central role. The spaces are shaped to feel warm, accessible and human, balancing social areas with quieter zones for focused work.

  • Tarsier

    Tarsier is a workplace with a strong identity, where materiality, light, and spatial structure come together in a cohesive and characterful environment.

  • Helo

    Helo is a calm and thoughtfully designed workspace where light, materials and proportions come together to support everyday work and meetings.

  • BBA

    BBA’s office is a carefully composed workplace where light, transparency and materiality create a calm, welcoming environment for everyday work.

  • Stratiteq

    Stratiteq’s office is a carefully composed workspace where analysis meets warmth. Light, materials, and structure support focus, dialogue, and presence.
