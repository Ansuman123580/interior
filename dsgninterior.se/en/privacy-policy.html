Privacy Policy

Information about how dsgn Interior collects, processes and protects personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

This Privacy Policy aims to inform the User about how this website processes personal data. It describes the types of data received or collected on the Website and the conditions under which they are used.

1. Definitions

A “User” refers to any natural person who accesses the website governed by this Privacy Policy.

“Personal Data” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, such as a name, location data, online identifier, or one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural, or social identity of that person.

2. Data Controller

dsgn interior is the data controller responsible for the processing of Personal Data in connection with the consultation and use of the Website.

3. Data Collected

The Personal Data collected directly from the User is strictly anonymous. It includes:

  • Source of access to the Website (search engine result, hyperlink from a third-party website, direct URL entry)

  • IP address (and therefore approximate geographical area)

  • Browser type

  • Operating system

  • Screen resolution

No data allowing direct identification of the User is collected.

4. Purpose of Processing

The User’s Personal Data is used exclusively for audience measurement purposes, in order to:

  • Analyze the effectiveness of the Website’s content

  • Assess display performance

  • Improve the browsing experience for future visitors

Under no circumstances will dsgn interior use this data for purposes other than those described above.

5. Data Recipients

The recipients of the personal data are, within the scope of their respective responsibilities:

  • The dsgn interior team

  • Technical service providers involved in the maintenance and operation of the Website (if applicable)

6. Data Retention Period

The collected Personal Data is retained only for the duration strictly necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected.

7. Refusal of Data Collection

You are free to prevent the collection of your Personal Data by configuring your web browser settings.
