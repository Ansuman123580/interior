Fellowmind’s office in Stockholm is a place where calm, clarity and function come together in a warm and welcoming work environment. Here, dsgn Interior has created an office that reflects the organisation’s values through considered material choices, soft forms and a clear sense of long-term thinking.

The Stockholm office is designed as a contemporary workplace where light, spatiality and materiality interact in a calm and cohesive whole. The ambition has been to create an environment that supports both focused work and collaboration, while allowing room for the changing needs of everyday working life without losing its distinct identity.

Daylight plays a central role in the design and shapes the experience of the spaces through soft textiles, muted tones and open sightlines. The structure of the office is clear yet never rigid, with rooms connected through a natural flow that creates clarity, calm and a sense of cohesion throughout the workplace.

Workstations, meeting rooms and shared areas are designed to support different ways of working throughout the day. Acoustic solutions and flexible furniture create conditions for concentration, dialogue and informal encounters, while a human scale has guided both proportions and furnishings.

Material choices are characterised by timelessness, quality and longevity, with details and surfaces that interact with the architecture and reinforce the overall impression. The result is a Stockholm office that combines function with warmth and structure with aesthetics, tailored to the people who use it every day.
