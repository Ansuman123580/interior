Fellowmind’s office in Gothenburg is designed as a calm and flexible work environment where clarity, functionality, and attention to detail come together. Shaped by the specific conditions of the site, the office supports focus, collaboration, and the changing rhythms of everyday work, in a setting where light, materiality, and spatial structure create a cohesive and welcoming whole.

Fellowmind’s office in Gothenburg is part of a coherent series of workplaces defined by a shared identity, while allowing each location to be shaped by its own context. The ambition has been to create a clear and recognisable concept that can translate across different cities, where local light, scale, and surroundings are allowed to influence the expression.

The Gothenburg office builds on this shared foundation through a consistent approach to spatial structure, materiality, and function. The design is calm and considered, with a balanced composition where colour palette, textiles, and daylight work together to create an environment that is both easy to navigate and comfortable to inhabit.

The layout allows for variation throughout the workday and supports different ways of working. Workstations, meeting rooms, and shared spaces are connected through a natural flow that encourages both concentration and spontaneous interaction. Acoustic solutions and flexible furniture contribute to a work environment that can adapt over time without losing its clear character.

The result is a Gothenburg office that clearly reflects Fellowmind’s overarching identity while remaining firmly rooted in its location. A flexible and long-term work environment where cohesion and local adaptation come together to support a functional and human-centred everyday experience.
