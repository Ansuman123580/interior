Fellowmind’s office in Jönköping is characterised by a welcoming and approachable work environment where everyday interaction plays a central role. The spaces are shaped to feel warm, accessible and human, balancing social areas with quieter zones for focused work.

The office in Jönköping was developed with the ambition of creating a workplace that functions as both a place to work and a place to gather. The spatial layout supports collaboration and informal meetings, while also offering opportunities to withdraw and focus when needed throughout the day.

Shared areas have been given a strong presence and are designed to encourage conversation, connection and a sense of belonging. Lounge spaces, meeting rooms and communal tables are linked in a way that allows the office to remain active and dynamic, creating natural movement and interaction between different parts of the workspace.

The material palette is warm and grounded, with textures and colours that contribute to a sense of continuity and comfort. Wood, textiles and soft lighting help create an atmosphere that feels inviting rather than rigid, supporting a work environment that is both functional and relaxed.

The result is an office that is clearly rooted in its location and context. A workplace that supports the rhythm of everyday work, where structure and flexibility come together to form a social, considerate and long-term environment for the people who use it.
