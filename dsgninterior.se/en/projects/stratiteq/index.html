Stratiteq

Stratiteq’s office is a carefully composed workspace where analysis meets warmth. Light, materials, and structure support focus, dialogue, and presence.

Location
Malmö, Sweden
Area
900
Year
2023

Stratiteq’s office is a place where analysis and reflection meet warmth and human presence. A work environment that balances precision with care, shaped to support focus, dialogue, and long-term thinking within a calm and cohesive whole.

The office is structured around clear spatial sequences where transparency and privacy are carefully balanced. Glazed meeting rooms, open work areas, and more intimate spaces for concentration are connected through a flow that encourages both collaboration and individual focus. Movement through the space is intuitive and legible, with sightlines that create connection rather than hierarchy.

Materials and colours are defined by a restrained elegance, where darker accents meet light timber, textiles, and soft daylight. Details such as lighting, furniture, and art are selected with care, contributing to an atmosphere that feels personal without becoming private, and representative without feeling formal.

The character of the existing building plays an active role in the design. Tall windows, generous proportions, and classic detailing are complemented by contemporary elements that give the office a distinct, modern identity. The result is an environment where historical qualities and contemporary expression coexist in a confident and balanced way.

Stratiteq’s office is designed to endure. A workplace where flexibility, sustainability, and quality are embedded in the structure, and where each space contributes to a whole that reflects the company’s way of thinking, working, and meeting—today and over time.
