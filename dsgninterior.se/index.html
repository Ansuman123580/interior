Modus Interior creates interior architecture for offices, hotels and public environments with a focus on function, aesthetics and sustainability.

Fellowmind Göteborg

Fellowmind’s office in Gothenburg is designed as a calm and flexible work environment where clarity, functionality, and attention to detail come together. Shaped by the specific conditions of the site, the office supports focus, collaboration, and the changing rhythms of everyday work, in a setting where light, materiality, and spatial structure create a cohesive and welcoming whole.

Car.info

Office interior design project for Car.info, focused on sustainable design, reuse and Scandinavian aesthetics, created by Modus Interior.

Stretch Care

Stretch Care’s new office brings energy and colour to a historic building in the heart of Malmö. By allowing the organisation’s values to meet the architecture’s heritage, the project creates a contemporary workplace where materiality, expression and well-being come together. A space shaped for people, identity and everyday presence.

Tarsier

Tarsier is a workplace with a strong identity, where materiality, light, and spatial structure come together in a cohesive and characterful environment.

Fellowmind Malmö

Fellowmind’s office in Malmö is a warm and considered workplace where materiality, daylight and spatial structure create a clear, human-centred whole.

Helo
